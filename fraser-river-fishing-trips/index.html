Good to know

Do I need a fishing licence?
Yes. Everyone fishing needs a BC fishing licence. Sturgeon trips also need a sturgeon tag. You only need a salmon tag if you plan to keep salmon.
How many people fit on a boat?
Four comfortably, five if needed. For bigger groups we add more boats.
Do you offer gift cards?
Yes. Gift cards are valid on any trip. Call or email us to set one up.
What time do trips start?
Usually 8am. We confirm the exact time when you book.
Do I need to bring gear?
No. Rods, reels, waders and boots are all provided.
What are the boats like?
Covered, heated aluminum jet boats, 21 to 24 feet long.
Where do we meet?
It depends on the trip. We confirm the launch location when you book.
Should I tip the guide?
Tips aren't included in the price. 10% is standard.

Questions before you book?

Big group, corporate day or a gift? Get in touch and we'll set it up.
